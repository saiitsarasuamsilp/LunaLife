What a session looks like

Four stages, always in this order. How long we spend in each one depends on your skin, not the clock.

1

Cleanse

A double cleanse to remove makeup, sunscreen, and surface oil before any heat touches your skin.

2

Steam

Open facial steam with a botanical blend chosen for your skin type, held at a comfortable, steady temperature.

3

Treat

Extraction if needed, then a mask or serum matched to what the steam revealed.

4

Cool down

A cool compress and light facial massage to close pores and calm the skin before you leave.
